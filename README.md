# Robert Eduard Antal

Head of AI & Automation at [Profilmedic](https://profilmedic.ro) · Founder of ZoominSEO (2019) · Cluj-Napoca, Romania

I build AI and automation systems that run in production. I have worked in search since 2008; today most of my work is making software answer, schedule and keep data fresh without someone watching it.

## What I work on

- **AI reception for medical practices**: voice and chat assistants that answer patients, with a hand-off to the practice when a human is needed.
- **A back office for doctors**: bookings, messages from every channel in one inbox, and calendar sync for doctors who work in several clinics.
- **Site builders and data pipelines**: the templates, content pipelines and infrastructure behind hundreds of sites, and data products such as daily exchange rates and auto-parts catalogues.

Most of this lives in private repositories. The live systems are listed, with links, on [antal.me](https://antal.me).

## Stack

TypeScript and Next.js, Astro, Python, PostgreSQL and Supabase, Cloudflare, Linux servers, LLM integration.

## Background

BA in Communication, Public Relations and Advertising, Babeș-Bolyai University (2026). My thesis studied how SEO professionals in Romania perceive and adapt to AI.
